First-time Android setup

Daily login address (laptop and phone)

The setup address only serves instructions and a public certificate. Enter passwords only at the HTTPS app address. Android certificate menus vary by phone; choose CA certificate, not Wi-Fi certificate.

Verify the certificate before installing

Compare this SHA-256 fingerprint with the phone setup page. The development CA is generated on your laptop; its private key is never shared.

Keep the laptop’s app and Docker Desktop running. Use headphones or put the phone in another room to avoid echo. Record and share tab audio from the laptop; the phone only needs to join.

Phone cannot connect?

Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi/hotspot and avoid guest Wi-Fi with device isolation. The laptop IP is detected automatically whenever start.ps1 runs, so after changing networks simply restart Meeting Brain and use the refreshed address shown above.
